# My Favorite Cartoon Series

## Gravity Falls

**Main Characters:**
- Dipper Pines
- Mabel Pines
- Grunkle Stan
- Bill Cipher

### Why I Like It
- Every episode has a mystery that makes me want to keep watching
- It mixes comedy and creepy moments perfectly
- I love the bond between Dipper and Mabel — they always support each other even when things get weird
- The show teaches lessons about family, friendship, and trusting yourself
- Bill Cipher is such an interesting villain — he’s funny but scary at the same time
- The hidden clues and codes make me feel like I’m part of solving the mystery
- The story feels meaningful and emotional especially near the end

**My Favorite Quote:**
> “Reality is an illusion, the universe is a hologram. Buy gold! Bye!” – Bill Cipher

## Regular Show

**Main Characters:**
- Mordecai
- Rigby
- Benson
- Pops
- Skips
- Muscle Man
- Hi-Five Ghost

### Why I Like It
- The stories always start normal but end up crazy in a fun way
- Mordecai and Rigby’s friendship feels very real — they mess up, but they always have each other’s back
- The comedy makes me laugh even if I’ve watched the episode before
- The animation style and characters are unique and memorable
- Even though it's funny, it also teaches lessons about responsibility, growing up, and friendship

**My Favorite Quote:**
> “Dude, bros stick together.” – Mordecai
